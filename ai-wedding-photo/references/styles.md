## 1. Japanese Film Natural

- ID: `japanese-film-natural`
- Chinese name: 日系胶片自然风
- Preview: `assets/style-previews/japanese-film-natural.jpeg`
- Mood: quiet, intimate, unposed, warm everyday nostalgia
- Setting: old streets, wooden homes, book rooms, kitchens, window light
- Palette: warm cream, faded green, wood brown, restrained black
- Light: soft morning or late-afternoon window light, gentle grain
- Wardrobe: simple ivory dress; understated dark suit
- Camera language: 35mm documentary photography, candid gestures, medium shots and environmental portraits
- Avoid: glossy studio finish, heavy retouching, ornate ballroom styling, exaggerated bokeh
